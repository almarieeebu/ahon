# Ahon — family & friends manual

**Live app:** https://ahon-app.vercel.app  

Ahon = *to rise* — a private money notebook for cash, tigum, utang, a simple month plan, and real spending.

---

## Quick start (phone)

1. Open https://ahon-app.vercel.app  
2. **Sign up** with your email (or Sign in if you already have an account).  
3. Answer three screens: pay and cash, usual bills, optional utang and a small cash cushion.  
4. When money moves, tap **Log money**.  
5. Use **Help** (top right) anytime. Full guide: **Menu → Profile → Open manual**.

Add the page to your phone Home Screen for an app-like icon.

---

## Everyday

You only need two jobs:

1. **Once:** what does this month usually look like?  
2. **When money moves:** log it.

| Action | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Payday | Home or Log → Money in |
| Gastos | Log money → amount + Food / Bills / Load / Transport / Other |
| Planned bill | Home → **I paid this** |
| Save to tigum | Menu → Tigum, or More details on Log |
| Bayad utang | Menu → Utang, or the Utang chip |
| Am I okay? | Home |

A weekly grocery total is enough. You do not need every jeepney fare.

---

## Budget

- The Budget tab opens on this month. Tap **Set monthly budget**, pick a category and a spending limit (add more if you like), and optionally expected income and bills with due dates.
- **Spent** and **Left** come from your Activity. You never type what you spent twice.
- Loan and card repayments have their own row. Things bought on credit count when you bought them.
- Spending in a category without a limit shows as **Unbudgeted spending**.
- Older budgets may show **Earlier manual entries (not from your activity)**: amounts typed by hand before. They are kept, never added to Spent. Choose **Keep as note** or **Hide** for each. Details: `docs/budget-migration.md`.

## Sharing

- Send the link + this manual.  
- Prefer **each person signs up** with their own email, **or** one household login you all trust.  
- Do not share passwords in chat.

---

## Tips

- Copy last month’s budget when a new month starts (Budget tab → Copy last month). Only limits, expected income and bills are copied.  
- Export backup: **Menu → Profile → Export backup**.  
- Replay the tour: **Menu → Profile → Replay tour**.
